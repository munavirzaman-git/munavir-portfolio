01 // Profile

About Me

I'm an Autonomous Vehicles Engineer from Kozhikode, Kerala, working across autonomous driving, ADAS simulation, vehicle control, and robotics — in both simulation and real-world environments. I hold an M.Tech in Robotics and Automation Engineering from the College of Engineering Trivandrum (CET).

I'm currently a Research Engineer at IIT Bombay on a Honda Cars India Ltd. sponsored project, building a CARLA-based autonomous driving pipeline — road and lane geometry extraction, LiDAR processing, object tracking, Stanley path tracking, and adaptive control using MRAC with Lyapunov-based parameter adaptation.

Before that, at IIT Palakkad with Mahindra & Mahindra, I developed ROS 2 navigation and control software for an autonomous agricultural tractor — field coverage planning, trajectory generation, GNSS/RTK + IMU + LiDAR integration, and controller evaluation through real field trials.

My foundation is vehicle kinematics, path planning, control systems, and sensor integration, written in Python and C++.
